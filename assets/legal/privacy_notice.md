# Fun App Ltd - Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 15 September 2026**

Fun App Ltd respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data.

This Privacy Notice explains how Fun App collects, stores, uses and protects your personal data when you visit its website, join its mailing list, submit an enquiry or register your interest in using or working with Fun App.

At this stage, Fun App’s website is primarily used to allow people to express an interest in the forthcoming app, join its mailing list or register a business, venue, club or charity’s interest in working with Fun App. Fun App will update this Privacy Notice if the way it processes personal data changes materially.

## 2. What personal data does Fun App collect?

If you join Fun App’s mailing list, it may collect your name and email address.

If you register a business, venue, club or charity’s interest in working with Fun App, it may collect information about the organisation and the person making the enquiry, including their name, role, email address and telephone number.

Fun App may also collect limited technical information about your visit to its website, such as your IP address, browser or device information, and information about how you interact with the website, where this is collected through cookies or similar technologies.

Fun App will only collect the information needed for the purposes described in this Privacy Notice.

## 3. How does Fun App use your personal data?

Fun App uses the personal data collected through its website primarily to:

- maintain a list of people interested in the forthcoming app;
- send emails about the app, including launch updates, announcements and other relevant news, where you have given the required consent;
- respond to questions or requests you send to Fun App;
- administer and protect Fun App’s website and systems;
- maintain records of marketing consent and withdrawals of consent; and
- comply with all legal and regulatory obligations.

Fun App will not use your personal data for purposes that are incompatible with the purposes described in this Privacy Notice unless it has a lawful basis or obligation to do so and, where it is required, will provide you with further information.

## 4. Information submitted by businesses, venues, clubs and charities

If you represent a business, venue, club, or charity and register your interest in using or working with Fun App, Fun App may collect the following information about you and your organisation.

Name and type of organisation (e.g., business, venue, club, or charity)
Structure (whether independent or part of a group/chain)
Number of locations (where applicable)
Venue or site capacity
Website address
Your full name
Your role or job title
Your professional email address
Your contact telephone number

Fun App collects this information to evaluate potential collaboration or partnership opportunities, respond to your enquiry and discuss how your organisation can use or work with Fun App.

### How Fun App uses business, venue, club and charity contact information

Where you register your organisation’s interest, Fun App may use the contact details you provide to contact you in relation to your enquiry.

Fun App may also keep a record of your enquiry and its communications with you where this is reasonably necessary to manage its prospective business relationship, maintain appropriate business records, protect its legitimate interests or comply with legal obligations.

For business, venue, club or charity enquiries, Fun App will rely on its legitimate interests in holding contact details in order to respond to enquiries, assessing potential commercial relationships and developing its business, provided that such interests are not overridden by your individual rights and freedoms.

Where Fun App relies on legitimate interests for this processing, it will consider the nature and purpose of the processing and balance its interests against your interests, rights and freedoms. Fun App will only process your personal data on this basis where it considers that the processing is appropriate and proportionate.

### Marketing communications to business, venue, club and charity contacts

Registering an interest in your organisation using or working with Fun App does not, by itself, mean that you have agreed to receive unrelated marketing communications.

If Fun App wishes to use your contact details for direct marketing beyond communications reasonably connected with your enquiry, it will ensure that it has an appropriate lawful basis to do so and complies with applicable direct-marketing laws, including the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR).

You can object to direct marketing at any time.

### Information about other people

Please only provide personal information about another individual where you are authorised to provide it and where your doing so is appropriate.

If you provide Fun App with another person's contact details, you should ensure that they are aware that their information has been provided to Fun App and that they may be contacted in connection with using or working with Fun App.

## 5. Fun App’s lawful basis for processing

Under the UK GDPR, Fun App must have a lawful basis for processing personal data.

### Marketing emails

Where you provide your email address to receive marketing emails from Fun App, it will rely on your consent for the relevant processing.

Fun App’s marketing emails may include information about the forthcoming app, launch announcements, developments, features, events and other news relating to the app.

It will obtain consent in accordance with applicable electronic marketing laws, including the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 ("PECR").

You can withdraw your consent to marketing at any time, including by using the unsubscribe link in any marketing email it sends you.

### Website administration and security

Fun App may process limited technical information where this is necessary to operate, secure and maintain its website and systems. Depending on the circumstances, its lawful basis may be its legitimate interests in operating and securing Fun App’s website and business, or another lawful basis available under applicable data protection law.

Where Fun App relies on legitimate interests, it will consider whether its interests are balanced against your interests, rights and freedoms.

### Legal obligations

Fun App may process personal data where this is necessary for it to comply with a legal or regulatory obligation.

## 6. Marketing emails

If you sign up to Fun App’s mailing list, it will only send you marketing emails where it has the appropriate permission to do so.

Where consent is required, Fun App will ask you to give that consent through a clear affirmative action.

Fun App will not use a pre-ticked box or rely on silence or inactivity as evidence of consent.

Fun App will keep appropriate records of marketing consent, including when and how consent was given and the scope of the consent.

Every marketing email it sends will identify Fun App and provide a straightforward way for you to unsubscribe.

If you unsubscribe from Fun App’s marketing emails, it will stop sending you marketing emails, subject to any limited processing necessary to ensure that it respects your unsubscribe request.

## 7. How long does Fun App keep your personal data?

Fun App will keep your name and email address for as long as you remain subscribed to its mailing list, unless it has another lawful reason to retain them.

If you unsubscribe, Fun App will remove your details from its active marketing list within a reasonable period.

It may retain a limited record of your unsubscribe request or withdrawal of consent where necessary to ensure that it does not send you further marketing communications.

Fun App may retain personal data for longer where this is necessary to comply with a legal obligation, establish or defend legal claims, or otherwise comply with applicable law.

Fun App regularly reviews the personal data it holds and will not retain that data for longer than is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

For business, venue, club or charity enquiries, Fun App will retain relevant contact information and records of its communications for as long as reasonably necessary to manage the potential or ongoing business relationship and for any further period required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

## 8. Who does Fun App share your personal data with?

Fun App may share your personal data with trusted third-party service providers where necessary to operate its website and mailing list.

These may include:

- website hosting and technology providers;
- email and mailing-list providers;
- IT, security and technical support providers; and
- professional advisers, where reasonably necessary.

Wherever a third-party processes personal data on its behalf, Fun App will put appropriate contractual arrangements in place as required by applicable data protection law.

Fun App may also disclose personal data where required to do so by law, regulation, court order or a lawful request from a competent authority.

Fun App does not sell users personal data or information about organisations enquiring, using or working with Fun App.

## 9. International transfers

Some of Fun App’s service providers may process personal data outside the United Kingdom.

Where Fun App transfers personal data outside the UK, it will ensure that the transfer is carried out in accordance with applicable UK data protection law and that appropriate safeguards are in place.

Depending on the circumstances, these safeguards may include an adequacy decision or appropriate contractual safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Further information about relevant international transfers can be obtained by contacting Fun App via the Privacy Contact detailed in section 1 above.

## 10. Cookies and similar technologies

Fun App’s website does not currently use analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies or similar tracking technologies to monitor your activity across its website.

Fun App may use cookies or similar technologies that are strictly necessary for the website to function, for example to provide basic website functionality, maintain security or prevent misuse.

Where a cookie or similar technology is strictly necessary for a service you have requested, Fun App may use it without obtaining consent where permitted by applicable law.

If Fun App introduces analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies or similar technologies in the future, it will update this Privacy Notice and, where required by applicable law, provide an appropriate consent mechanism before operating those technologies.

You will be able to manage your choices in accordance with the options provided at that time.

## 11. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you have rights under UK data protection law including:

- the right to ask for a copy of the personal data Fun App holds about you;
- the right to ask Fun App to correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data;
- the right to ask Fun App to erase your personal data in certain circumstances;
- the right to ask Fun App to restrict the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances;
- the right to object to certain processing;
- the right to withdraw consent where Fun App relies on consent as its lawful basis; and
- in certain circumstances, the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format.

You also have an absolute right to object to the processing of your personal data for direct marketing purposes.

If you withdraw your consent to marketing, this will not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before you withdrew your consent.

Some rights are subject to exceptions or limitations under applicable law.
